# Social Media & Mental Health Analysis - Tableau Dashboards

## 📌 Project Overview
This project explores the relationship between students' social media habits, their academic commitments, and their overall mental health. Through interactive Tableau dashboards, we analyze platform preferences, time-spent patterns, and how excessive usage correlates with stress levels.

## 📊 Dashboards Included

### Dashboard 1: Social Media Usage Overview
**Objective:** Understand platform preferences and time spent patterns among students.

**Key Visuals:**
* **Donut Chart:** Distribution of social media platforms (Instagram, WhatsApp, YouTube, TikTok, etc.)
* **Bar Chart:** Average hours spent per platform.
* **Bar Chart:** Usage category comparison (Entertainment, Education, Networking, News).
* **KPI Cards:** Average daily usage hours and the percentage of heavy users (>4 hours).
* **Interactive Filters:** Age | Gender | Platform.

### Dashboard 2: Stress & Mental Health Analysis
**Objective:** Explore how social media usage correlates with stress levels and academic demands.

**Key Visuals:**
* **Scatter Plot:** Study Hours vs. Mental Health Score among different academic levels.
* **Heat Map:** Stress levels categorized by Age × Gender.
* **Bar Chart:** Average stress score by social media platform.
* **Geographical Map:** Country-based mental health scores.
* **KPI Cards:** Percentage of students reporting high stress and the average stress score.
* **Interactive Filters:** Platform | Age Group | Gender | Stress Level.
